Dependencies are installed by the user; their binaries are not included in the source ZIP.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| PyMuPDF | 2.1 | AGPL-3.0 or commercial license, as declared by upstream | [pymupdf/PyMuPDF](https://github.com/pymupdf/PyMuPDF) |

The tool uses the open-source dependency distributions. Upstream copyright and license notices remain with the installed packages.

- PyMuPDF's licensing information is available in its installed `COPYING` file and [official documentation](https://pymupdf.readthedocs.io/en/latest/about.html#license-and-copyright).
- The AGPL license text is available in this project's [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [from GNU](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html).
